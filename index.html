A day with Wellness Companion

Three small moments, each one a few minutes long.

  1. Morning

    Set a gentle intention

    Pick one thing you'd like to feel or focus on before the day gets busy.

  2. Midday

    Notice what's draining you

    A quick check-in helps you spot what's taking more from you than it gives.

  3. Evening

    Name one good thing

    Close the day by noticing what went well, even if it was small.

Built around three promises

01

Short by design

Five minutes is enough. The goal is consistency, not a perfect journal entry.

02

Honest about its limits

Wellness Companion supports reflection. It isn't therapy, diagnosis, or medical care.

03

Yours to control

Your check-ins are for you. [Add your real data and privacy commitments here.]

If you need more than a check-in

If you're in crisis or might hurt yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline right away. A companion can help you reflect, but it can't replace people who can be with you.
